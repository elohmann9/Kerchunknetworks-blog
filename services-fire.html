Redundancy · Failover
Dual SIM & Starlink Failover

A single carrier going down shouldn't take your apparatus dark. Dual SIM keeps you on air when one carrier fails. Add Starlink at the station and you have a third path — critical for rural departments where LTE coverage isn't a guarantee.

  • Dual SIM configuration — AT&T and Verizon simultaneously
  • Automatic failover with zero manual intervention
  • Starlink installation and station integration
  • Load balancing across available connections
  • Failover testing and validation
  • Coverage mapping for your response area
