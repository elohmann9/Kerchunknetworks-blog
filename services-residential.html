Security · Network Design
VLAN Segmentation

Your smart TV doesn't need to talk to your laptop. VLAN segmentation keeps your devices separated by trust level — main devices on one network, IoT gadgets isolated on another, guests on their own lane with no access to anything that matters.

  • Main network for trusted devices
  • IoT VLAN — cameras, smart home, TVs
  • Guest network — isolated, internet only
  • Firewall rules between VLANs
  • Device assignment and labeling
  • Documented network map on handoff
