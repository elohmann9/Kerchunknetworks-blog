Best Practices

🔐

Use Strong WiFi Passwords

Keep the Kids network password different from your main network. Consider changing it periodically to maintain control over which devices connect.

📊

Monitor Usage Reports

Review UniFi's built-in analytics to see which sites and apps are being accessed. Look for patterns that might indicate bypasses or concerning activity.

⏰

Weekend Flexibility

Consider creating separate weekend schedules with extended access hours. This teaches time management while allowing appropriate leisure time.

🛡️

DNS-Level Protection

Use family-friendly DNS servers like CleanBrowsing (185.228.168.168) or OpenDNS Family (208.67.222.123) for additional content filtering.
