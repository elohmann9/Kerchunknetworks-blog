About Kerchunk Networks

Our Mission

To provide reliable, secure connectivity for those who protect our communities — and the families who support them.

We believe technology should reduce stress, not create it. Every system we design prioritizes stability, clarity, and usability so teams can focus on operations, not troubleshooting.

About Kerchunk Networks

Kerchunk Networks provides professional network engineering, deployment, and support services designed specifically for emergency response environments, remote operations, and modern households.


Founded on firsthand experience in the fire service, Kerchunk Networks understands that connectivity is no longer a convenience — it is a critical component of operations, safety, and communication. From incident response and station infrastructure to mobile deployments and home networks, our solutions are built to perform when reliability matters most.


We specialize in designing and implementing secure, resilient network systems that support real-world conditions, including limited connectivity areas, mobile command environments, and high-availability requirements. Every network we deploy is purpose-built, carefully documented, and configured for long-term reliability and scalability.

What Sets Us Apart

Unlike traditional IT providers, Kerchunk Networks focuses on operational environments where failure is not an option.


Our approach combines:

  • Fire-service insight — Built with an understanding of emergency operations, shift work, and field deployments
  • Enterprise-grade equipment — Including Cradlepoint, Starlink, UniFi, cellular failover, and GPS-enabled systems
  • Mission-ready design — Redundancy, uptime monitoring, and secure remote access
  • Clear documentation — So departments know exactly how their systems function
  • Long-term support — Not just installation, but ongoing reliability

We don’t sell generic networks. We design infrastructure that supports people who serve their communities.
